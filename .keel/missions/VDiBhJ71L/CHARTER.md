# Research-Preserving Bearing Promotion - Charter

Archetype: Strategic
## Goals

| ID | Description | Verification |
|----|-------------|--------------|
| MG-01 | Make `keel bearing lay` carry research forward automatically into epic planning artifacts so bearing brief content, findings, risks, and sources survive the promotion step without lossy manual restatement. | board: VDiHwPniZ |

## Constraints

- Preserve provenance from `BRIEF.md`, `EVIDENCE.md`, and `ASSESSMENT.md` when generating epic planning artifacts.
- Keep generated PRD content editable and explicit instead of hiding research carry-forward in opaque metadata.
- Do not silently drop risks, open questions, or cited sources during promotion.
- Maintain one canonical promotion path instead of optional side scripts or manual copy workflows.

## Halting Rules

- DO NOT halt while laying a bearing still requires lossy manual re-entry of research into the epic PRD.
- HALT when `keel bearing lay` scaffolds epic planning from bearing research with explicit provenance and minimal manual copy-paste.
- YIELD to human if automatic carry-forward exposes unresolved ambiguity in the epic PRD contract itself.
